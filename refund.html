Refund Policy

Last Updated: [5-7-26]

At Crafted Core, many of our products are customized and made according to the information provided by the customer.

Because customized products are created specifically for an individual order, we follow a specific refund and replacement policy.

Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

1. Customized Products

Customized products are generally not eligible for return, refund or cancellation due to change of mind, personal preference or incorrect customization details provided by the customer.

This may include incorrect:

  • Name
  • Text
  • Spelling
  • Photo
  • Design
  • Size
  • Color
  • Product selection
  • Other customization information

Customers are responsible for carefully reviewing their order details before completing the purchase.

2. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective or unusable due to a manufacturing or fulfilment issue, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

We may ask you to provide:

  • Order ID
  • Photographs of the product
  • Photographs of the packaging
  • Video evidence where necessary
  • A description of the issue

Our team will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

Depending on the circumstances, we may offer:

  • Replacement
  • Reproduction/correction of the product
  • Refund
  • Another appropriate solution

3. Wrong Product or Incorrect Order Fulfilment

If you receive a product that is different from the product you ordered due to an error on our part, please contact us promptly.

After verification, we will work to provide an appropriate resolution.

4. Cancellation

Order cancellation requests must be made as soon as possible after placing the order.

Once a customized product has entered the design, customization, production or fulfilment process, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If cancellation is accepted before production begins, the applicable refund will be processed according to the circumstances and payment method.

5. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method where possible.

The time taken for the refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment gateway, bank or financial institution.

6. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, where applicable, may be non-refundable unless the refund is due to an error attributable to Crafted Core or another situation where we determine that the shipping amount should also be refunded.

7. Return Shipping

If a return is approved, we will provide instructions regarding how the product should be returned.

Do not send a product back without contacting us first.

Unauthorized returns may not be accepted.

8. Situations That May Not Qualify for Refund

A refund or replacement may not be available in situations including:

  • Customer changes their mind
  • Customer provides incorrect customization details
  • Customer enters incorrect spelling or name
  • Customer selects the wrong product, size or color
  • Product is damaged after delivery due to customer handling
  • Normal variations in color, print, engraving or material
  • Minor differences caused by screen/display settings
  • Customer refuses delivery without a valid product-related reason
  • Customer is unavailable to receive the package
  • Incorrect or incomplete shipping information provided by the customer

9. Delivery Issues

If an order is returned to us because of an incorrect address, incomplete address, recipient unavailability or repeated failed delivery attempts, additional shipping charges may apply if re-shipping is requested.

10. How to Request a Refund or Replacement

Please contact Crafted Core through our official support channel and provide:

  • Order ID:
  • Customer Name:
  • Registered Phone/Email:
  • Reason for Request:
  • Photos/Videos: Where applicable

Our team will review the request and respond with the next steps.

11. Policy Changes

Crafted Core may update this Refund Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
