Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Crafted Core FAQ section. Here you'll find answers to some of the most common questions about our products, customization, orders, shipping and returns.

Products & Customization

We offer a variety of customized and personalized products, including T-shirts, wallets, lockets, mugs, bottles, bracelets, posters and other gifting products.

Our collection may change and expand over time.

Yes. Many of our products are designed specifically for customization.

Depending on the product, you may be able to add a name, text, photo, design or other personalization details.

Please check Customize Yours Now for the customization options available.

If a product supports custom design requests, you can contact our support team before placing your order.

We will let you know whether the requested customization is possible.

Where applicable, we may provide a preview or confirmation before production.

However, previews may vary slightly from the final physical product due to screen settings, printing processes, material characteristics and production conditions.

Orders

Simply select your desired product, choose the available options, enter your customization details and add the product to your cart.

After reviewing your order details, proceed to checkout and complete the payment.

If your order has not yet entered the customization or production process, we may be able to make certain changes.

Please contact us as soon as possible after placing your order.

Once production has started, changes or cancellations may no longer be possible.

Customized products are prepared specifically according to the customer's requirements.

Therefore, cancellation may not be possible once customization or production has started.

Please refer to our Refund Policy for complete details.

Payments

Available payment methods are displayed during checkout.

Payment must be successfully completed for prepaid orders before the order can be processed.

Payments are processed through the payment methods or payment service providers available at checkout.

Crafted Core does not intentionally store your complete payment card details.

Shipping

Delivery time depends on the product, customization requirements, processing time and delivery location.

Estimated shipping information may be displayed during checkout or on the product/order page.

Customized products may require additional processing time.

Where tracking is available, tracking details may be shared with you after your order has been dispatched.

Delivery timelines can occasionally be affected by courier delays, weather, holidays, operational issues or circumstances beyond our control.

If your order appears significantly delayed, please contact our support team with your order details.

Please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

For damaged, defective or incorrectly delivered products, we may request photographs or videos showing the product and packaging so that we can investigate the issue.

Returns & Refunds

Because customized products are made specifically according to your requirements, returns may generally not be accepted for change of mind, personal preference or incorrect customization details provided by the customer.

Please read our Refund Policy before placing an order.

If your product arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us promptly.

After reviewing the issue, we may offer an appropriate resolution such as replacement, correction or refund, depending on the circumstances.

Customers are responsible for checking their customization information before submitting an order.

If incorrect information is provided by the customer and production has already started, we may not be able to make changes or provide a refund.
