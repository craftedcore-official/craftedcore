Shipping & Returns

Last Updated: [5-7-26]

At Crafted Core, we work to prepare and dispatch your order as efficiently as possible while ensuring that customized products are created according to your requirements.

1. Order Processing

After an order is successfully placed and payment is confirmed, we begin processing the order.

Customized products may require additional time for personalization, quality checking and packaging.

Processing time may vary depending on:

  • Product type
  • Customization requirements
  • Order volume
  • Availability
  • Design approval, where applicable
  • Operational circumstances

2. Shipping

Once your order is ready, it will be handed over to the applicable delivery partner.

Estimated delivery times may vary based on the delivery location, courier service and other circumstances.

Delivery estimates are indicative and are not guaranteed.

3. Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information may be provided after your order has been dispatched.

Customers should use the tracking information to monitor the shipment and remain available to receive the package.

4. Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed due to circumstances beyond our control, including:

  • Courier delays
  • Weather conditions
  • Public holidays
  • Transportation issues
  • High-volume periods
  • Incorrect address information
  • Regional restrictions
  • Operational disruptions

Such delays do not automatically qualify an order for cancellation or refund.

5. Incorrect Address or Contact Details

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information at checkout.

This includes:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • House/building information
  • Street/locality
  • City
  • State
  • PIN code

If an order cannot be delivered because incorrect or incomplete information was provided, additional shipping charges may apply for re-shipping.

6. Failed Delivery

If a customer is unavailable to receive the order or repeatedly refuses/does not accept delivery, the shipment may be returned to us by the courier.

Re-shipping may be possible depending on the circumstances and may involve additional shipping charges.

7. Damaged Package

If the package appears visibly damaged at the time of delivery, please document the condition of the package and product with photographs or video where possible.

Contact us promptly so that we can investigate the issue.

8. Wrong or Damaged Product

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or defective product due to an issue on our side, please contact us as soon as possible.

We may request photographs, videos and order details for verification.

After reviewing the issue, we may offer a replacement, correction or refund as appropriate.

9. Returns

Because many Crafted Core products are personalized and made specifically according to customer requirements, returns are generally not accepted for customized products due to change of mind or personal preference.

Returns may be considered for eligible cases such as an incorrect product being sent or a verified product defect attributable to us.

Please contact us before sending anything back.

10. Return Authorization

Do not ship a product back to us without receiving return instructions from our team.

If a return is approved, our support team will provide the applicable return instructions.

11. Non-Returnable Situations

Products may not be eligible for return because of:

  • Change of mind
  • Incorrect customization details supplied by the customer
  • Incorrect spelling/name supplied by the customer
  • Wrong size or color selected by the customer
  • Personal preference
  • Minor color differences caused by screen settings
  • Normal product/material variations
  • Damage caused after delivery by improper handling

12. Shipping Charges on Returns

Where a return is approved, the responsibility for return shipping will depend on the reason for the return and the circumstances of the order.

We will communicate the applicable instructions when your return request is reviewed.
