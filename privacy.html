Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [5-7-26]

At Crafted Core, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, place an order or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Order details
  • Product customization information
  • Information you voluntarily provide to us
  • Customer support communications
  • Information required to process or deliver your order

For customized products, the information you provide may include names, messages, photographs, designs or other content required to create your order.

2. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

Depending on the payment method used, payment providers may collect and process payment-related information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Crafted Core does not intentionally store complete debit card, credit card, banking or payment credentials on its own systems.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders
  • Create customized products
  • Arrange shipping and delivery
  • Communicate with you about your order
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Process returns, replacements or refunds where applicable
  • Improve our website, products and services
  • Prevent fraud, misuse or unauthorized activity
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements

4. Customized Content

If you provide photographs, names, messages, logos or other information for customization, we use that information only as reasonably necessary to fulfil your order and provide the requested service.

Please do not submit content that you do not have permission to use.

You are responsible for ensuring that the photographs, designs, names, logos or other content you submit do not violate the rights of another person or organization.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers when required to operate our business, including:

  • Payment service providers
  • Courier and delivery partners
  • Website or hosting providers
  • Technology and service providers
  • Customer support providers
  • Government or legal authorities where required by law

We do not sell your personal information as a product to third parties.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to provide essential website functionality, understand website usage and improve the user experience.

Cookies may also be used by certain third-party services integrated into the website.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including order processing, customer support, accounting, legal and regulatory requirements.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, payment providers, social media platforms or other services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below.
